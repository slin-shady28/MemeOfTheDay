Chromebook help

How to post from school

These steps work in Chrome on a school Chromebook without installing anything.

1

Open Post

Go to the Post tab and add your display name plus a meme pic name.

2

Browse web images

Search for a school-safe image, open it, and copy the image address.

3

Paste the link

Put the image address in Paste image URL, or make a text-only meme.

4

Post and vote

Post it, then check the Memes and Leaderboard tabs.

Safety tab

Before you post

Keep it funny, safe, and okay for school.

Good to post

School-safe jokes, clean reaction pictures, class-friendly text memes, and images your teacher would be okay seeing.

Do not post

Private info, mean posts, unsafe links, bad language, or anything about a classmate that could embarrass them.

Best rule

If you would not show it to your teacher, do not post it here.

Rules tab
